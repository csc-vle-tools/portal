# 🚀 CSC VLE Tools — High-Performance Cyber Cafe & VLE Portal

**CSC VLE Tools** is a free, fast, and 100% offline-capable client-side utility suite built for Village Level Entrepreneurs (VLEs), Jan Seva Kendra operators, and Cyber Cafe owners.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Utilities Included (22 Flat Modules)

* 📄 **Document Generators:**
  * Marriage Biodata Maker (`tools_biodata_maker.html`)
  * Resume / CV Builder (`tools_resume_builder.html`)
  * Job Cover Letter Generator (`tools_cover_letter_generator.html`)
  * Rent & Service Agreement Format Generator (`tools_agreement_format_generator.html`)
  * Salary Slip Generator (`tools_salary_slip_generator.html`)
  * Utility Bill Payment Receipt Generator (`tools_bill_receipt_generator.html`)
  * Invoice Generator (`tools_invoice_generator.html`)

* 🎴 **Card & Photo Printing Utilities:**
  * Single & Multiple ID Card / PAN / Aadhaar Alignment Tool (`tools_pan_aadhaar_print.html`, `tools_id_card_print.html`, `tools_multiple_id_card_print.html`)
  * Passport Photo Sheet Generator (4x6 Photo Paper & A4 Grids) (`tools_passport_photo_sheet.html`)
  * Rubber Stamp Maker (`tools_csc_stamp_maker.html`)

* 🧮 **Calculators:**
  * Exam Cut-Off Age Calculator (`tools_age_calculator.html`)
  * Percentage & Marks Calculator (`tools_percentage_calculator.html`)
  * CGPA to Percentage Converter (`tools_cgpa_calculator.html`)
  * Loan EMI Calculator (`tools_loan_emi_calculator.html`)

* 🛠️ **Image & PDF Processing:**
  * Photo & Signature Resizer with KB Compressor (`tools_photo_resizer.html`)
  * PDF Merger & Image-to-PDF Engine (`tools_pdf_merger.html`)
  * Image Text Extractor (OCR) (`tools_image_text_extractor.html`)
  * Instant QR Code Generator (`tools_qr_code_generator.html`)

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Performance

All tools run **100% locally in the browser**. No client data, photos, documents, or personal information are ever uploaded to any external server.
